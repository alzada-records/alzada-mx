MAHO

MAHO is a Mexican electronic music duo that performs live, made up of Gabriel Montaño and María José Garza. Hailing from Guadalajara, Mexico, the project explores house and other electronic genres, giving its productions their identity through Spanish-language vocals. MAHO has been supported by artists such as Musumeci, Alican, Jimi Jules, Âme and Dixon.

In their debut year, 2024, MAHO reached Beatport’s Top 100 with the EP “Sabes Que Sí,” released on Roam Recordings. In 2025, they teamed up with Musumeci on the EP “Me Sientes,” released on Anims (Barcelona) along with a remix by Pole Position.
