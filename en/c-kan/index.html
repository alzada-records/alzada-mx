C-Kan

C-Kan is currently the leading Mexican artist in his genre, with more than 50 official videos on VEVO, some of them with over 100 million views. He has headlined major festivals around the world such as Urbano Fest, Prudence Fest, Revolution Fest, the Budweiser Made in America Festival and the High Times Cannabis Cup. He has also collaborated with international artists such as Baby Bash, Frankie J, South Park Mexican, Alika, Kinto Sol and Kevin Gates.
