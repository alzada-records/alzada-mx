Almanegra

Almanegra is a Colombian artist who has steadily built his presence in the urban scene with an authentic sound that fuses rap, trap and alternative music. His style stands out for its dark aesthetic, introspective lyrics and well-defined identity that connects with new audiences across Latin America. With constant releases and strategic collaborations, he is an emerging project with international reach.
