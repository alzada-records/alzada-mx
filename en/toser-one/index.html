Toser One

Toser One is a Mexican rapper who has become one of the most representative figures in Mexican hip hop, known for his direct style, authentic attitude and strong connection to the streets. His music mixes classic rap with contemporary sounds while keeping a solid identity within the scene.

Throughout his career, he has built a loyal fan base through consistency, raw lyricism and his live shows, taking part in many collaborations and projects that have made him a leading name in independent rap in Mexico.
