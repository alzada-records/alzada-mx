[RAZÓN SOCIAL PENDIENTE] (“Alzada”), located at [DOMICILIO PENDIENTE], is responsible for the use and protection of your personal data. This notice applies to alzada.mx and ladrones.mx.

2. What data we collect

Only what you give us in our forms:

We don’t sell or rent your data. It is stored with service providers that help us run the site: Google (spreadsheets and email) and Netlify (website hosting), whose servers may be outside Mexico. If your request involves an artist (for example, a booking), we may share it with the artist or their management team. We may also share it with authorities when required by law.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw your consent. Email us at [CORREO PENDIENTE] with your name, the email you used and what you need. We’ll reply within 20 business days.

6. Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe at any time on the Unsubscribe page or with the link in every email.
