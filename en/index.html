    About

    Alzada was born in Guadalajara, Jalisco, to open doors for emerging talent in an industry full of barriers and recycled formulas. What started as an independent movement is now a creative ecosystem built on authenticity, the streets and a direct connection with a new generation.

    From the very beginning, it has bet on artists who are now leading names in the urban scene across Mexico and Latin America. With audiovisual production, digital content, brand partnerships and live experiences, Alzada is more than a label: it’s a 360 platform that keeps breaking the mold and raising the bar for music in Latin America.

    Sync / Licensing

    At Alzada, sync and licensing turn music into a strategic asset. Our catalog brings together authentic, versatile artists ready for films, series, campaigns, video games and digital content, adding identity, emotion and value to every project.

    We work in an agile, curated way, handling rights, clearances and deliverables so projects move without friction. From picking the right track to closing the license, we align creativity with business goals, helping brands, agencies and production companies reach new audiences through music.
