Keysie SM

Born on September 5, 2014, Keysie SM (Keysie Sauceda) has urban music in her DNA. The daughter of the beloved and iconic Mexican rapper Lefty SM, Keysie grew up surrounded by beats, bars and the unique passion of recording studios. But her music isn’t about repeating the past: it turns her heritage into a movement of her own, fresh and fully forward-looking.

Representing a new generation that doesn’t believe in rigid labels, in 2026 Keysie begins a new chapter at Alzada, the label where her father shined. In this new stage, she explores a versatile sound that moves freely between urban pop and contemporary R&B, combining catchy melodies, modern beats and a fresh vibe full of creative freedom.

Directly influenced by global figures like Young Miko, Keysie SM stands out for her authentic attitude, youthful style and a natural ability to connect with listeners her age. Her music reflects the energy of today’s youth: free, stylish, honest and full of rhythm.

Keysie SM doesn’t just honor her family legacy with pride; she arrives on the scene ready to make her own mark, bringing fresh air to Spanish-language urban music.
