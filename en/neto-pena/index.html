Neto Peña

Neto Peña is a Mexican rapper and songwriter known for his melodic style and emotional lyrics that blend hip hop, trap and urban music. From Guadalajara, Jalisco, he has become one of the most prominent voices in Mexico’s independent scene.

Over his career, he has built a strong fan base thanks to his authenticity and versatility, writing about love, heartbreak and everyday life. With many singles and notable collaborations, Neto Peña keeps rising as an artist in the Latin urban music landscape.
